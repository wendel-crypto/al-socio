Al Socio

Home services in Miami run on WhatsApp and word of mouth. Al Socio gives it structure: verified, licensed pros with upfront prices, booking in three taps, live tracking and a neighbourhood community, in English and Spanish from day one.

The problem

Finding a pro you can trust still takes three group chats.

TRUST. Homeowners cannot tell a licensed plumber from someone with a van. Licences, insurance and reviews live in different places, if they exist at all.

SUPPLY and PRICING. Good pros are booked through referrals and never list prices, so every job starts with a quote round-trip and ends in haggling.

RETENTION. Once the job is done, the relationship disappears into a phone contact. Al Socio keeps it: saved pros, one-tap rebooking and a community feed where neighbours show real work.

What's in it

Trust built into every step.

01

Verified, licensed pros

State licence number, insurance, background check, completion rate and response time on every profile.

02

Upfront pricing

Per-service prices and hour estimates before you book. Nothing is charged until the job is done and approved.

03

Three-step booking

Job and photos, a two-hour arrival window, then review with promo code and Apple Pay. ASAP adds a priority fee.

04

Live job status

Pending, confirmed, on the way with a live ETA, in progress, done and paid, with reschedule and cancel built in.

05

Neighbourhood community

Pros post before/after work, neighbours post reviews that tag the pro, and every post has a Book button.

06

Bilingual by default

Every screen, message and notification in English and Spanish, switchable live, because that is how Miami talks.

Model & metrics

18% on completed jobs. Zero for a pro’s first 90 days.

The take rate only applies when a job is completed and paid, and new pros pay nothing for 90 days, which removes the main objection to joining a marketplace while supply is thin. The metrics that decide whether it works:

Design system

Night navy, one warm action colour.

Dark navy keeps photos of real work in front. Coral marks every action (book, send, post), teal means verified, gold means ratings and offers, and every service category has its own colour so the home grid scans at a glance. Nunito for UI text, JetBrains Mono for every number and price.
